## Data Collection and Use
Finansio is an offline-first application. **We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell your personal data or financial information.** 

All financial records, categories, and settings you enter into the app are stored locally on your device using a local SQLite database. We do not have access to your data, and we do not use any external servers to store or process your personal information.

## Local Storage and PDF Reports
The app requires storage permissions solely for the purpose of saving local database files and generating PDF reports of your financial data directly to your device's local storage. These files never leave your device unless you manually choose to share them.
